> ITCH.IO NEWS

  • [ BUNDLE ] 324 Games for $10: itch.io's Games for Scholarships Charity Bundle Is Live Through September 20

    Organizer jesthehuman's back-to-school bundle collects everything submitted to the Games for Scholarships jam — 365 entries across video games, browser games and tabletop RPGs, made between July 13 and August 10 under a jam policy that barred discriminatory and AI-generated material. The resulting DRM-free package is 324 items from 159 creators for a $10 minimum, against a regular value of roughly $673. Every dollar goes to Games for Change, funding scholarships for underserved youth in the G4C Student Challenge, the nonprofit's global design competition for students aged 10 to 25. Collection opened August 17 and runs to September 20; as of this morning it has raised $2,224 of a $10,000 goal from 186 contributors, who are averaging $11.95 each — comfortably above the asking price. Source: itch.io bundle page · Jam page

  • [ TOOLS ] Pixelorama Turns Seven and Ships v1.2.1 With SVG Export and Custom Godot Blit Shaders

    Orama Interactive's free, open-source pixel art multitool — built in Godot, downloadable from itch.io and runnable straight in the browser — posted its August 19 devlog only a few weeks after the big v1.2 release. The team calls 1.2.1 a small update focused on bug fixes and polish, but it lands real features: basic SVG export (every non-transparent pixel becomes its own rect element, with same-color pixel merging planned later to shrink file sizes), a new Corner Pin layer effect, support for loading your own texture blit shaders, a much more powerful Index Map effect, right-click keyframe deletion, tool shortcuts that fire with the cursor off-canvas, and timeline quality-of-life work like track folding and cursor snapping. The devlog also marks the project's seventh birthday: what began as a 2019 experiment now ships with splash art commissioned from artists like Roroto Sic, alongside optional paid builds on Steam and itch.io for anyone wanting to fund development. Source: itch.io devlog · Tool page

  • [ DEVLOG ] Aura Studios Pushes Buried Below's Launch to August 21 After a Rough Steam Review

    The atmospheric survival-horror devlog posted August 18 confirms the game — where a camping trip goes wrong and you descend into an abandoned mine to find a missing girlfriend, managing lighter fuel and stealth against what's lurking below — now launches Friday, August 21 at 19:00 CEST for €4.99. It was originally due August 12, but "unexpected setbacks" during Steam's approval process pushed it back over a week; the team thanked everyone "who were ready to play on August 12" for their patience. Closing line: "The tape is finally ready to start playing again. The memory awaits." Source: itch.io devlog · Game page

  • [ DEVLOG ] Solo Dev Finishes Killer Trait's Demo-Closing Chapter 4 While Relocating Argentina to Spain

    Prikarin's August 17 devlog on the dark visual novel — where protagonist Fawna chooses whether to ally with a serial killer for revenge, across mystery, romance and LGBTQIA+ threads — confirms Chapter 4, the longest chapter and the free demo's endpoint, is fully written and edited. Three of four new backgrounds and one of five new CGs are finished, with three more CGs added late to land the climax; character work on Fawna's ally Oz is still in progress. Past the demo, players split into one of three routes based on how Fawna manages her anger, each ending in Restoration or Destruction for six endings total. The solo creator, coordinating commissioned artists, voice actors and an editor while settling into a new country, is targeting a final demo for November 6 and a full launch in 2027–2028 around $12. Source: itch.io devlog · Game page

> INDIE SCENE

  • [ FESTIVAL ] Steam's Tiny Teams Festival Is Back, Spotlighting 200+ Games From Five-Person Studios or Smaller

    Yogscast Games' week-long Steam event opened August 6 and runs through August 13, gathering 200-plus games from solo developers and studios of five core members or fewer — publishers don't count against that cap, so plenty of the featured titles have real backing despite tiny credits lists. The pitch is a dedicated Steam event page plus daily coverage across the main Yogscast Twitch and YouTube channels and the wider Yogscast network, mixing discounts on established indie bestsellers with playable demos for upcoming ones. GameGrin and other outlets flagged Peak, RimWorld and Baby Steps as the names most likely to pull newcomers in, with the festival's actual job being everything smaller sitting next to them on the front page. Source: The Gamer · The Geekly Grind

  • [ STEAM REVIEW ] Molleindustria's Future? No Thanks! Held Back by Valve Nudity Review

    The July launch of Molleindustria's most ambitious project — a narrative, open-world adventure set in an ecosocialist 2045 where your grumpy elder is scheduled for euthanasia — slipped after Valve's build review ran long over, in the developer's words, "dozens of untextured polygons depicting ass and boobs." The dev noted the identical demo passed review months ago; the Steam page still reads "Coming soon." The radical-games studio behind Phone Story and the McDonald's Videogame has a free demo out in the meantime. Source: Molleindustria · Steam

> INDIE GAME RELEASES — AUGUST 2026

  • AUG 20 Mortal Shell II — OUT TODAY

    Cold Symmetry's standalone sequel to its 2020 soulslike breakout unlocks today at 10:00 Pacific: possess warrior Shells, dethrone false gods and drag a ravaged world back from ruin through high-stakes, adrenaline-forward combat. Devout Edition buyers have been in since August 17 on 72 hours of advanced access, and the pre-launch open beta pulled more than a million players across Steam, PS5 and Xbox. $49.99 standard / $59.99 Devout, with a physical PS5 Revered Edition for collectors. PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Steam · DLCompare

  • AUG 20 Mexican Ninja — OUT TODAY

    Bucharest's Amber Studio sends its 2.5D roguelike beat 'em up out across PC and consoles today. Nuevo Tokyo is run by the Narkuzas — a feudal ruling class formed when Mexico's narcos and Japan's yakuza merged — and you join the rebels plotting against its five clan heads. Combat leans on precise movement, positioning and timing, layered with spirit powers and outlandish "Mexican Jutsus," while randomized runs, meta-upgrades and a skill tree called the Way of the Donkey carry progress between deaths. A free demo remains up on Steam. PC via Steam, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Steam · Saving Content

  • AUG 19 Project P.I.T.T. — OUT NOW

    Solo developer Froke's incremental physics-automation sandbox puts you in a brutalist PSX-styled corporate facility with one task: manufacture rubber ducks and feed them to a bottomless pit. Production escalates from there into absurd contraptions built out of magnets, fans and other workplace-approved equipment, chasing corporate targets and colossal combos while the Maw grows and the question of who exactly you work for goes unanswered. The game racked up 10 million-plus YouTube views on organic creator coverage and neared 75,000 wishlists before launch; published by Pretty Soon, also behind FEED IT and Backpack Hero. 15% off for the first two weeks. PC via Steam. Steam · GamingOnLinux

  • AUG 19 Hearth and Hamlet — OUT NOW

    Phorust Studios' cozy incremental citybuilder starts you at a single campfire and grows it into a full medieval kingdom: assign workers to gather resources, build up from market stalls and town halls to barracks, banks and magic academies, and lean on deep upgrade trees for a relaxing idle-management loop. Published by Runic Forge off a free Steam demo that drew more than 100,000 lifetime players, with the game itself passing 150,000 wishlists ahead of launch. PC via Steam. Steam · GAMESTIC

  • AUG 18 Last Pirates: Die Together — Early Access

    Ukrainian studio RetroStyle Games' 1-4 player co-op horror comedy leans on chaotic ragdoll physics and ridiculously stretchy arms: haul as much booty as you can back to your captain while monsters — and the same teamwork keeping you alive — turn your own crewmates into hazards. Entered Steam Early Access off a demo that pulled 300,000-plus players and nearly 200,000 wishlists. PC via Steam. Steam · GamingBible

  • AUG 18 Twisted Tower — OUT NOW

    Atmos Games' Thomas Brush (Pinstripe, Neversong) ships his studio's first full-3D project after nearly five years: a first-person, narrative-driven BioShock-inspired horror-adventure where you scale a lethal, floor-by-floor tower inside a forgotten 1950s amusement resort — waterpark, clown casino, carnival forest, space station and luxury hotel — to rescue a lost love. Published by 3D Realms, built by a small team including artist Felipe Caravelli and programmer Ajay Venkat. PC via Steam. Steam · Gematsu

  • AUG 18 The Sinking City 2 — OUT NOW

    The Lovecraftian survival-horror sequel drops players into a Roaring '20s Arkham half-swallowed by flooded, drowned alleys after an eldritch deity's awakening. Players defy the entity's influence while exploring the submerged city, managing sanity against cosmic horrors, and piecing together the mystery behind the flood. Launched with advance access a day early for pre-purchasers, plus a standing Steam demo. PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Steam · MonsterVine

  • AUG 18 Entropy — Early Access

    Dread Delusion studio Lovely Hellplace swaps first-person action for tactical turn-based battles in this JRPG-inspired dark fantasy: command up to six characters from a theater troupe turned demon-fighters in the dying, infertile world of Draenog, managing positioning and resources through the first of three planned acts. Published by DreadXP, off a demo covering the opening hours. Early Access is expected to run roughly a year. PC via Steam. Steam · Noisy Pixel

  • AUG 17 Tukoni: Forest Keepers — OUT NOW

    Kyiv studio Dream Operator's hand-drawn cozy adventure casts you as Traveller, a forest spirit exploring magical hand-painted locations, meeting charming characters, solving handcrafted puzzles and gathering herbs and tea recipes to help the forest through the coming winter. The free demo built more than 66,000 wishlists ahead of launch and holds 97% positive reviews on Steam; the full game already picked up Excellence in Visual Art at the DevGAMM Awards 2025 and Best Upcoming Game Art Style at the Digital Vikings Awards 2026. PC via Steam and Epic Games Store. Steam · Tech Times

  • AUG 17 FEED IT — OUT NOW

    Italian studio Games On A Chair's commercial debut is a short-form, PSX-styled psychological horror piece pitched as Mouthwashing by way of Silent Hill: trapped inside a building with an abusive, demanding monster, you obey, scavenge food to hand over, eat it yourself and face the consequences, or try to escape — a restrained, minimal-interaction structure built for multiple paths and endings over replayed runs rather than one long campaign. Published by Pretty Soon, the studio behind Backpack Hero and Havendock. PC via Steam. Steam · Saving Content
