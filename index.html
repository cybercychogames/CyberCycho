> ITCH.IO NEWS

  • [ BUNDLE ] Hardship Fund Bundle Closes Today Having Raised $244K — 97% of Its $250K Goal

    Necrosoft Games' itch.io bundle for the United Videogame Workers–CWA Local 9433 hardship fund is down to its final hours: $244,290.55 raised from 17,960 contributors, 97% of the $250,000 target, with the offer closing today, August 14. The fund pays laid-off and under-employed developers in the US and Canada directly, through two tiers ($1,000 and $1,000–$5,000) reviewed independently by the union rather than by Necrosoft or organizer Brandon Sheffield. 127 DRM-free items — A Short Hike, SkateBIRD, Cook, Serve, Delicious!, They Bleed Pixels, Wheels of Aurelia, Lucah: Born of a Dream, NEON STRUCT and more, worth roughly $1,023 combined — remain $10 for anyone who wants in before the countdown hits zero. Source: itch.io · GameSpot

  • [ FROM ITCH.IO ] Solo Dev's itch.io Incremental Void Salvage Graduates to Steam

    Myra D'Ann Boholst posted the devlog this week: what started on itch.io as a small, audio-less incremental with just the core loop in place has shipped on Steam as a full release under the BohFam banner. Void Salvage is a one-hand-and-a-drink space roguelite auto-battler — scavenge derelict ships, fight off waves through an auto-resolving combat system, and gradually rebuild a vulnerable starting ship into an overpowered warship loaded with lasers, lightning weapons and drones across a 62-node respec-anytime skill tree. The Steam version adds multiple ship and weapon combinations, meta progression, strange signals, hostile pursuits, bosses and a full story on top of what the itch.io demo had. $6.99 with a 15% launch discount. Source: itch.io devlog · Steam

  • [ MILESTONE ] Dwarf Fortress Turns 20 — Kitfox Shows the First Art From Its Magic Update

    Patch 53.16 doubles as an anniversary card: twenty years after Tarn and Zach Adams put the first version online, Kitfox Games is marking it with a livestreamed show and the first public look at what comes after the current era of updates — Myth & Magic. New art from Thea Kent depicts "a dwarven wizard, a bunch of wild looking creatures and the God of Blood Armok himself," and composer Dabu paired with Simon Swewer on an accompanying track about "a dwarven wizard experimenting with the amazing, destructive powers of magic." The pair are taking the update to Gamescom at the end of the month for a first public gameplay showing, streamed for anyone not in Cologne. The anniversary show itself airs on Kitfox's Twitch channel with Tarn and Zach playing the original 2006 build next to the current one, plus the first-ever Beyond Quality Awards, honoring "the devs and community members who have contributed throughout the years." The patch underneath the celebration is mostly cleanup: unicorns and other fanciful creatures are back with properly tracked materials, tavern patrons are capped at one drink so taverns stop turning into medical emergencies, library visitors stop walking off with the books, and procedurally generated weapons and armor finally render with the materials they're actually made of. Source: itch.io devlog · Game page

  • [ JAM ] B1T Jam 5 Voting Tops 1,800 Ratings on Its Sleep-Themed Entries

    Host Macrow's 10-day, 2-colors-only jam is deep into its rating window: entries have settled at 175 and the community has logged 1,817 ratings so far, with winners across Judges' Pick, Community Vote and the design/art categories still unannounced. The theme this round reads more like a riddle than a prompt: "a periodic state of reduced awareness, closed eyes, and relaxed muscles... a term for dormancy, numbness, or a gentle euphemism for death" — interpretation left entirely to the entrant. The rules that made B1T Jam 4 distinctive carry over unchanged: only two visible hex colors on screen, no alpha tricks that sneak in a third, and generative AI "prohibited, No exceptions." Web builds aren't mandatory, but skipping one disqualifies an entry from Judges' Pick — the other awards are community vote, best-designed game page and best cover art, each worth a year of Craftpix and a front-page feature. Source: itch.io

> INDIE SCENE

  • [ FESTIVAL ] Steam's Tiny Teams Festival Is Back, Spotlighting 200+ Games From Five-Person Studios or Smaller

    Yogscast Games' week-long Steam event opened August 6 and runs through August 13, gathering 200-plus games from solo developers and studios of five core members or fewer — publishers don't count against that cap, so plenty of the featured titles have real backing despite tiny credits lists. The pitch is a dedicated Steam event page plus daily coverage across the main Yogscast Twitch and YouTube channels and the wider Yogscast network, mixing discounts on established indie bestsellers with playable demos for upcoming ones. GameGrin and other outlets flagged Peak, RimWorld and Baby Steps as the names most likely to pull newcomers in, with the festival's actual job being everything smaller sitting next to them on the front page. Source: The Gamer · The Geekly Grind

  • [ STEAM REVIEW ] Molleindustria's Future? No Thanks! Held Back by Valve Nudity Review

    The July launch of Molleindustria's most ambitious project — a narrative, open-world adventure set in an ecosocialist 2045 where your grumpy elder is scheduled for euthanasia — slipped after Valve's build review ran long over, in the developer's words, "dozens of untextured polygons depicting ass and boobs." The dev noted the identical demo passed review months ago; the Steam page still reads "Coming soon." The radical-games studio behind Phone Story and the McDonald's Videogame has a free demo out in the meantime. Source: Molleindustria · Steam

  • [ SPOTLIGHT ] Xbox Indie Selects — August 2026

    The ID@Xbox team's six hand-picked indies for August: Deer & Boy (Dear Villagers, $19.99), Hell Clock (Mad Mushroom, $24.99), D-topia (Annapurna Interactive, $19.99), Cozy Grove: Camp Spirit (The Quantum Astrophysicists Guild, $19.99), Backrooms: Escape Together (Triiodide Studios, $19.99, up to six-player co-op) and Moss: The Forgotten Relic (Polyarc, $19.99), the flatscreen adaptation of the VR duology. Source: Xbox Wire

> INDIE GAME RELEASES — AUGUST 2026

  • AUG 14 Lootbound — OUT NOW

    ArtDock's tactical roguelite starts every run with a single hero and builds outward: recruit companions who fight on their own volition during turn-based battles, gear up on loot-driven decisions, and chain items and synergies into a full expedition party across a dark fantasy world with D&D-flavored trappings. The team has multiple post-release updates already in development. Launching with a 35% discount, off the back of a free demo. PC via Steam. Steam · GameGrin

  • AUG 14 Static Dread: The Submarine — OUT NOW

    solarsuit.games follows up its Lovecraftian sim Static Dread: The Lighthouse with a claustrophobic sequel set aboard the military submarine Ares, trapped in the North Atlantic after a catastrophic aurora erases much of modern civilization. As the captain's right hand you assign crew tasks, analyze sonar signals and make hard calls to keep everyone from madness while something older than any map closes in. Published by Polden Publishing, off a demo that drew a positive reception. PC via Steam. Steam · Bloody Disgusting

  • AUG 14 I.T. Never Ends — OUT NOW

    Dadbod Games' dark-comedy desktop sim casts you as the newly hired Systems Administrator at a company that was "definitely founded in 2015" — the printer is bleeding, the Wi-Fi is haunted, and Ticket #666 just came in. You survive the night shift through binary decisions across a branching story with multiple endings, all rendered through full 3D backgrounds and a cast of hand-drawn portraits. Started as a solo itch.io project before crossing 20,000 Steam wishlists ahead of launch. PC via Steam. Steam · itch.io page

  • AUG 13 Servant of the Lake — OUT NOW

    Rusty Lake's newest point-and-click puzzle adventure is a prequel set decades before Rusty Lake: Roots, back inside the Vanderboom family manor. You start a new job as a servant at the estate, solving hand-crafted puzzles and uncovering the family's dark alchemical secrets across the series' signature surreal, unsettling atmosphere. Originally aimed at spring 2026, the developers pushed the launch to August after roughly doubling the volume of both story and puzzles. Releasing simultaneously on Android, iOS and Steam, off the back of a playable demo on all three. Steam · Rusty Lake blog

  • AUG 13 Wild Blue Skies — OUT NOW

    Chuhai Labs and VITEI Backroom's on-rails arcade shooter revives the genre's '90s heyday: you fly Bowie Stray and the Blue Bombers over oceans, desert ridges and perilous caves, blasting through waves of enemies to push back Grimclaw's forces alongside a cast of allies. Controls are built to be easy to pick up for genre newcomers while still rewarding veterans, wrapped in a hand-crafted, anime-style universe. Published by Balor Games, off a free Steam demo that's been up for months. PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. Steam · Gamers Heroes

  • AUG 12 Pro Jank Footy — OUT NOW

    Powerbomb Games and Tinker Town's nostalgic comedy sports roguelike bounces onto every platform at once: an over-the-top arcade take on Australian football with two teams of 18 and more than 150 power-ups that warp the rules mid-match. Play local multiplayer for the chaos or a solo season mode for something closer to a real career, with online play alongside both. Published by Powerbomb Games and Umbrella Games. PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 1|2. Steam · Vooks

  • AUG 11 AI Apocalypse — Early Access

    A cyberpunk roguelike deckbuilder built around three weapon classes — plasma sword, arc staff, rail rifle — nine build paths and more than 120 cards. Runs layer stance combat and distance management on top of deckbuilding fundamentals, sending you through three procedurally-generated acts against an adaptive AI enemy while managing shields, armor and gear synergies. The developers have committed to landing at least one community-sourced change in every weekly patch during Early Access. PC via Steam. Steam · Official site

  • AUG 11 Stonewards — Early Access

    Banana Tiger Studio's 1-4 player co-op first-person roguelite defense game sends a crew of dwarves out from their base camp to aid nearby kingdoms: mine deep for resources, forge upgrades and hold the line against relentless monster attacks. The Early Access run is planned to last nine to twelve months. $9.99, PC via Steam. Steam · GameGrin

  • AUG 11 Omen — OUT NOW

    Codex Interactive's debut is a top-down sci-fi extraction shooter: you play an Astral, an immortal starship pilot investigating The Veil, a cosmic anomaly that's rallied an army of cultists toward total war. Missions send you looting the war-torn remains of a fallen galactic empire and shoring up a defensive front against pirates, traitors and cultists alike — grab the objective, then decide whether to stage a quick exit or dig in when the enemy response fleet shows up, capital ships included. Over 80 weapons, defensive structures, abilities and utility items combine into hundreds of loadouts, layered under a four-act, Lovecraftian-tinged story where each act adds a new enemy faction and closes on a boss fight. PC via Steam. Steam · Saving Content

  • AUG 10 Cult of PiN — OUT NOW

    Solo developer KennyMakesGames shipped a pinball-roguelike that splits the difference between Windows' old Space Cadet table and the deckbuilding craft of Balatro or Peglin: clear four hand-drawn 3D tables by shattering bumpers, hitting mission targets and cracking gravity-defying Boss Bumpers, then draft between rounds from a pool of 130-plus upgrades to build toward a run-ending score. Over 100 Glyphs and Blessings chain into combo multipliers, and 8 playable balls each carry their own passive mechanic. Native support for Windows, Mac, Linux and Steam Deck, off the back of a free demo that pulled in more than 120,000 players. Steam · Gamezebo
